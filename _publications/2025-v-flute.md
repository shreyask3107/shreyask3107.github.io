V-FLUTE studies how vision-language models interpret figurative meaning in images and text. The expert-verified dataset contains 6,027 examples spanning metaphors, similes, idioms, sarcasm, and humor, with entailment labels and explanations.

The work combines automatic and human evaluation to examine where visual-language reasoning succeeds and where it breaks down.
